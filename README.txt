ASHRAFUL ISLAM — EXECUTIVE PORTFOLIO

LOCAL PREVIEW (WINDOWS):
1. Download and extract this ZIP file.
2. Open the folder ashraful_islam_portfolio_final.
3. Double-click index.html. It opens in Chrome or your default browser.
4. Keep the assets folder beside index.html; do not move images out of it.

VPS DEPLOYMENT:
Upload the contents of this folder (index.html, styles.css, script.js, assets/) to your website document root, such as public_html or your Nginx web root.

The Best Buy gallery uses the four photographs supplied for this portfolio. Captions describe visible activities only; no dates or performance figures were invented.

THEME TOGGLE & COMPANY LOGOS:
- Use the Light mode / Dark mode toggle in the top navigation. Your preference is saved in the browser.
- Supplied company marks are shown beside the matching roles in the career timeline.
- The ACI Logistics role uses the supplied Shwapno logo image; the image is the Shwapno retail brand mark, not a standalone ACI corporate logo.
